Our Core Business

Machining & Dies Mold

High-precision manufacturing of industrial equipment and components. We have the ability to design and make machines upon customer's request.

  • WELE CNC Machining Center
  • CAMPRO CNC Vertical Machining
  • HISION VMC
  • SKM T60 Standard ZDM Machine

Surface Treatment & Finishing

Plating used to decorate objects, for corrosion inhibition, to improve solderability, and alter conductivity.

  • Nickel Plating (Ni)
  • Copper Plating (Cu) & Zinc Plating
  • Buffing & Trimming

  • Cuttingwell & Hand Grinding
